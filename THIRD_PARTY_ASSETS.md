# Third-party assets used by HELLGATE

- [Kenney Modular Space Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-space-kit) — room, door, and ceiling meshes. Creative Commons CC0.
- [CC0 Flat Guns West](https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-flat-guns-west) by Pichuliru — pistol, rifle, and SMG models. Creative Commons CC0. Files: `assets/third_party/flat_guns_west/`.
- [Blood splat](https://opengameart.org/content/blood-splat) by TobiasM — blood spray and floor stain texture. Creative Commons CC0. File: `assets/third_party/gore/blood_splat.png`.
- [Lost in a bad place](https://opengameart.org/content/lost-in-a-bad-place-horror-ambience-loop) by congusbongus — exploration ambience. Creative Commons CC0. File: `audio/third_party/lost_in_a_bad_place.ogg`.
- [Ambience Pack 1 -- Sci Fi Horror](https://opengameart.org/content/ambience-pack-1-sci-fi-horror) by Joth — combat ambience and an additional unused track. Creative Commons CC0. Files: `audio/third_party/infestation_control_room.mp3`, `audio/third_party/depths_of_hell.mp3`.
- [Abandoned Spaceship Godot Demo](https://github.com/perfoon/Abandoned-Spaceship-Godot-Demo) by Jaanus Jaggo (Perfoon) — consoles, containers, damaged arcade cabinets, barrels, and debris. Assets are Creative Commons BY-NC 4.0; code is MIT. The licence text is in `ABANDONED_SHIP_LICENSE.txt`. **Commercial use of these assets requires permission from the author.**
- [GodotFPS-Template](https://github.com/bukkbeek/GodotFPS-Template) by bukkbeek — original FPS player, zombie, weapons, effects, and sounds. See the repository's `LICENSE` and `README.md` for its credits and dependencies.
